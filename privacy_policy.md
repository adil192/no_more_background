# NoMoreBackground's Privacy Policy

NoMoreBackground (henceforth, "the App")
is an optimization tool for Android™ and allows the user to
control their apps' permissions, developed by Adil Hanney.

Android is a trademark of Google LLC.

## Data types

The App only accesses data that is strictly necessary for its function,
including your list of apps and their permissions.

No data is shared or collected off your devices.

## Data deletion

To delete any data used by the App, simply uninstall it.
